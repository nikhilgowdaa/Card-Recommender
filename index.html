Key terms, caps, and claim conditions

HSBC Live+ Credit Card 10% / 1.5%

10% Accelerated Cashback (5 Categories): Dining, Food Delivery, Grocery & Quick-Commerce (MCC 5411/5812/5814), Shopping (Apparel, Sports/Decathlon MCC 5941, Electronics MCC 5732, Cosmetics MCC 5977, Dept Stores MCC 5311), and Utilities/Telecom (MCC 4814/4900 e.g. Jio, Airtel, Electricity, Gas, Water).

Cap: ₹1,200 per statement cycle on 10% categories. Explicit Exclusions from 10% Shopping: Direct Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra spends earn 1.5% base cashback.

Swiggy HDFC Credit Card 10% / 5% / 1%

10% on Swiggy: Food orders, Instamart, Dineout, Genie (Max ₹1,500/mo cap).

5% on Select Online Partners: Decathlon, Nykaa, Ola, Uber, Pharmeasy, 1mg, Ajio, BookMyShow (Max ₹1,500/mo cap). Note: Amazon & Flipkart purchases usually earn only 1% base cashback due to MCC variations.

Kotak 811 Super Debit Card (+ GyFTR & Apay Routes) 5%

1. Direct Online & POS (5%): Valid on eligible retail POS swipes and select online checkouts (Direct Kotak 811 DC checkout on Amazon & Flipkart does NOT give 5% — use GyFTR by Kotak route instead).

2. GyFTR by Kotak Brand Vouchers (5% — Required for Amazon & Flipkart): Buy Amazon & Flipkart vouchers (plus Zepto, Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit, Myntra, Nykaa, Uber, etc.) on GyFTR by Kotak to earn 5% cashback.

3. Amazon Pay Gift Card → Wallet Route (5%): Buy Amazon Pay Gift Cards on GyFTR by Kotak (5% CB) → Load into Amazon Pay Balance → Pay in any online app accepting Amazon Pay Wallet OR pay Utility Bills & Recharges (Electricity, Water, Gas, Broadband, Jio/Airtel) on Amazon.

Strict Cap: Max ₹100 per transaction (₹2,000 spend = ₹100 CB), ₹500 per month. Must be manually claimed in the Kotak 811 app before month-end.

Amazon Pay ICICI Credit Card 5% / 3% / 2% / 1%

5% Unlimited (Prime) / 3% Unlimited (Non-Prime): Amazon spends (toggle Prime membership in Manage My Cards). 2%: Bill payments & 100+ Amazon Pay partner checkouts. 1%: All other retail spends (including Flipkart). No monthly caps.

Supermoney pro Axis Credit Card 3%

3% Cashback: On UPI checkouts and top partner categories.
