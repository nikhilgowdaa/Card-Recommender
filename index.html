Key terms, caps, and claim conditions

HSBC Live+ Credit Card 10% / 1.5%

10% Accelerated Cashback: Dining, Food Delivery, Grocery & Quick-Commerce (MCC 5411/5812/5814 e.g., Zepto, Blinkit, Swiggy, Zomato, DMart), and Utilities/Telecom (MCC 4814/4900 e.g. Jio, Airtel, Electricity).

Cap: ₹1,200 shared monthly cap on 10% categories. Exclusions from 10%: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra earn 1.5% base.

Swiggy HDFC Credit Card 10% / 5% / 1%

10% on Swiggy: Food orders, Instamart, Dineout, Genie (Max ₹1,500/mo cap).

5% on 1000+ Online Partners: Amazon, Flipkart, Decathlon, Nykaa, Ola, Uber, Pharmeasy, 1mg, Ajio, BookMyShow (Max ₹1,500/mo cap).

Kotak 811 Super Debit Card (+ GyFTR & Apay Routes) 5%

1. Direct Online & POS (5%): Valid on eligible online checkouts and retail POS swipes.

2. GyFTR by Kotak Brand Vouchers (5%): Buy brand gift cards (Zepto, Flipkart, Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit, Myntra, Nykaa, Uber, etc.) on GyFTR by Kotak to earn 5% cashback.

3. Amazon Pay Gift Card → Wallet Route (5%): Buy Amazon Pay Gift Cards on GyFTR by Kotak (5% CB) → Load into Amazon Pay Balance → Pay in any online app accepting Amazon Pay Wallet OR pay Utility Bills & Recharges (Electricity, Water, Gas, Broadband, Jio/Airtel) on Amazon.

Strict Cap: Max ₹100 per transaction (₹2,000 spend = ₹100 CB), ₹500 per month. Must be manually claimed in the Kotak 811 app before month-end.

Amazon Pay ICICI Credit Card 5% / 2% / 1%

5% Unlimited: Amazon spends for Prime members. 2%: Bill payments & 100+ Amazon Pay partner checkouts. 1%: All other retail spends. No monthly caps.

Supermoney pro Axis Credit Card 3%

3% Cashback: On UPI checkouts and top partner categories.
